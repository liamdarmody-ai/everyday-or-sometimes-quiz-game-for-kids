🪜 Tier Time: health is a ladder, not a pass/fail test

There are no letter grades and nobody fails. Foods live on different rungs, how they're made can move them a rung, and favourite foods live on every rung — yes, even the top rung and the Sometimes rung. Your job is just to learn where each one lives, and why.

💪 Power Fuel — top rungProtein, fiber, vitamins, water — foods that do the most for you
⚡ EverydayEnergy staples you can eat every day, best with protein & veggies beside them
🔀 MiddleIn between — how it's made or what comes with it moves it a rung
🎉 Sometimes rungTreats for once in a while — still favourites, just not everyday fuel
